Tender documents, the Central Supplier Database and many corporate clients still ask for a "tax clearance certificate". SARS replaced that certificate with the **Tax Compliance Status (TCS)** system: you request a PIN on eFiling and share it, and the other party checks your status directly with SARS. If your status is red on the day they check, the tender can be lost, so it pays to understand what drives it.

## What the PIN is

SARS describes TCS as a system that lets you obtain a PIN in real time. A third party, such as a tender office, another business or a government entity, uses your **tax reference number and PIN** to view your compliance status. Your status is not fixed: it changes with your tax behaviour.

For organisations, the request type is **Good Standing**, which SARS describes as confirming that your tax affairs are in order. (The other type, Approval International Transfer, is for individuals transferring funds abroad.)

## How to request it on eFiling

1. Log in to eFiling and activate the Tax Compliance Status service (once only).
2. Open **My Compliance Profile**. If you have several tax types, merge them first.
3. Choose **Tax Compliance Status Request**, select Good Standing, and set how many months the PIN should be valid.
4. If your tax affairs are in order, the PIN is issued immediately by SMS or email.

## Green or red: what SARS checks

| Requirement | What SARS's guide says |
| --- | --- |
| Registration | You must be registered and active for the tax types you are liable for |
| Returns | No returns you are liable for may be outstanding after their due dates |
| Debt | No outstanding tax debt for which a payment arrangement has not been made |

**Green** means compliant; **red** means non-compliant. On eFiling you can hover over the non-compliant indicator to see the reason.

## The most common reasons for a red status

- An old VAT201, EMP201 or EMP501 that was never submitted, often from a period when the business was quiet.
- A company income tax return (ITR14) outstanding for a prior year.
- A small balance on one tax type, such as penalties or interest, with no payment arrangement.
- The business is liable for a tax type, for example PAYE, but not registered for it.

Note the wording on debt: a debt covered by an agreed payment arrangement does not make you non-compliant on that requirement. That is why a payment arrangement can matter before a tender deadline.

## If you believe your status is wrong

SARS provides a **Challenge Status** option at the bottom of the My Compliance Profile screen. It is available only when non-compliance is shown, and you cannot lodge a new challenge while one is in progress. SARS states that if it agrees, a limited-time override may be applied to your profile. You can also fix the underlying problem through the normal SARS channels.

## Plan ahead of tenders

1. Check My Compliance Profile **monthly**, not only when a tender is advertised.
2. Fix outstanding returns early: SARS has to process them before the status changes.
3. Keep a payment arrangement in place for any debt you cannot settle.
4. Request the PIN with a validity period that covers the tender evaluation, and request a new one if it expires.
