Who we are

Think Mybiz Accountants & Tax Practitioners supports South African businesses and individuals with accounting, taxation, payroll and statutory compliance. We also assist taxpayers facing SARS queries, disputed assessments and outstanding tax debt. Our engagements connect the books, tax returns and supporting evidence so that each submission has a clear basis.

Leadership and professional background

The practice is led by MF Banda, director and registered tax practitioner. His professional background includes Tax Technician (SA), Chartered Business Accountant in Commerce and Certified Financial Accountant designations, and an Occupational Certificate in Tax Technician at NQF Level 6.

Our purpose and approach

Our purpose is to make accounting and tax compliance understandable and manageable. We explain what is required, agree the scope and fees, and keep records of the work completed. We aim to build lasting client relationships through accurate work, confidentiality and direct communication.

Digital service with practitioner oversight

Taxnify is our base accounting and compliance software platform. Electronic records and remote meetings support our service, while professional judgement, review and client authorisation remain central to each engagement.

Serving clients throughout South Africa

Based in Mokopane, Limpopo, with remote services available in all nine provinces, including Johannesburg and Midrand, Pretoria and Polokwane. See how we work, our services, or what to do if you have received a SARS letter.
