Accounting and tax help in Pretoria

Many Pretoria clients come to us for SARS matters: verification letters, disputed assessments and tax debt, as well as tax clearance and CSD requirements for government and tender work. Because the work is done remotely, you do not need to travel; documents are sent electronically and meetings happen by phone or video call.

All work is prepared and reviewed under MF Banda, SARS registered tax practitioner (PR-0034776), and nothing is submitted to SARS or CIPC without your approval.
