Ways to engage us

Retainer, once-off or dispute matter

Services are available through monthly retainers, once-off assignments or separate dispute and recovery engagements. The quotation identifies deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities and fees; external approval and third-party charges are addressed separately.

Confidentiality and client responsibilities

Working together properly

We handle personal and financial information with regard to POPIA and professional confidentiality. Clients provide complete and accurate records, approve submissions as agreed and make payments by the required dates. Access and authority are arranged through the applicable taxpayer and practitioner processes.

Remote by design

No office visit needed

Documents are sent electronically and filed in a dedicated client folder per financial year. Work is prepared on Taxnify and reviewed by the practitioner before anything is submitted, and only with your approval.
