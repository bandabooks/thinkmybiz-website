SARS disputes & tax debt

Received an assessment, audit letter or final demand?

A disputed assessment requires the correct procedure, supporting evidence and careful deadline management. We review the facts and the available remedy before preparing a response, from a request for correction or an objection to payment arrangements and suspension of payment.

  • Penalty and interest remission

Common questions

Frequently asked

Do you only work with clients in Limpopo?

No. Our head office is in Mokopane, Limpopo, but we serve individuals and businesses in all nine provinces, including Johannesburg, Pretoria and Midrand, through remote service. Office consultations are available by arrangement.

How do I send you my documents?

Electronically. We agree a secure route for sensitive documents, file them in a dedicated client folder per financial year, and meet by phone or video call.

How much do your services cost?

Fees depend on the work. After a free consultation we send a written quotation that identifies the deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities and fees before any work starts.

Who will do my work?

Work is prepared and reviewed under MF Banda, director and SARS registered tax practitioner (PR-0034776). Nothing is submitted without your approval.

I received a letter from SARS. What should I send you?

Send the full notice, the assessment date, the relevant tax type and period, and any existing case reference. Ask us for a secure route before sending sensitive supporting documents.
