CSD registration step by step for small businesses

How to register on the government Central Supplier Database, the information you need, how tax details are checked, and how to stay active.

By MF Banda, SARS registered tax practitioner PR-0034776 · Published 20 November 2026

If you want to sell goods or services to national, provincial or local government, registering on the Central Supplier Database (CSD) is the starting point. The CSD is the single database that government uses for supplier information, and suppliers register themselves online.

What the CSD does

The CSD keeps a database of organisations, institutions and individuals who can provide goods and services to government. Government departments use your CSD supplier number to view your verified information, which is why the details you capture, especially tax and company details, must be correct and current.

Before you start: have this ready

Section What you will need
Identification Supplier type (for example, a CIPC-registered private company or close corporation), registration number, turnover band and financial year-end
Address Physical address details
Bank account Account holder name exactly as on the bank confirmation letter, account type, number, bank and branch code
Tax Income tax reference number, and VAT number if registered
Directors or members Each director's contact details and shareholding
Commodities The goods and services you supply, and whether you supply province-wide or nationwide
B-BBEE Your B-BBEE status details

Step by step

  1. Go to www.csd.gov.za, register a user account and activate it through the email and one-time PIN you receive.
  2. Log in, accept the terms and choose Add supplier.
  3. Complete identification: supplier type, registration number and business details.
  4. Choose your industry classification.
  5. Add your address.
  6. Add your bank account and request the one-time PIN to confirm it.
  7. Capture your tax details.
  8. Complete directors or members.
  9. Select your commodities and the area you supply.
  10. Complete B-BBEE details.
  11. Submit. You receive a CSD supplier number, which starts with MAAA, and a unique registration reference number.

Tax compliance is checked against SARS

Government departments view your verified tax information through the CSD, which draws on SARS, so a non-compliant status on SARS eFiling will count against you when a department checks your CSD report. Before you rely on your CSD registration for a tender, make sure your returns are up to date and any SARS debt is paid or under a payment arrangement. Our article on why a tax compliance status shows non-compliant explains what SARS checks.

Keep your profile active

Registration is not a once-off task:

  • update bank, address, director and contact details whenever they change;
  • keep your company details current at CIPC: the Western Cape Government reports that CIPC and the CSD have been integrated to verify beneficial ownership compliance; and
  • respond to any request from a government department or provincial treasury to update your profile.

Where to get help

National Treasury's CSD helpdesk can be reached at csd@treasury.gov.za or 012 406 9222, and provincial treasuries also assist suppliers with registration.
