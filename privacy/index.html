Privacy

Privacy & POPIA notice

How this website handles personal information.

Think Mybiz Accountants & Tax Practitioners (Think Mybiz Accountants and Tax Practioners (Pty) Ltd) respects your privacy and processes personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect through this website

When you send an enquiry, we receive the details you choose to give us: your name, email address, phone number, province and town, the service you are interested in and your message. The enquiry is sent by email to accounts@thinkmybiz.co.za; the website does not store it in a database.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quotation and, if you engage us, deliver the agreed services and meet our legal and professional obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Sensitive information

Please do not send identity numbers, tax reference numbers or supporting documents through the website form. Ask us for a secure route for sensitive documents.

Client information

Client information is handled with professional confidentiality. Access and authority to act with SARS and other authorities are arranged through the applicable taxpayer and practitioner processes.

Your rights

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its processing, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations. Contact us at accounts@thinkmybiz.co.za. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
