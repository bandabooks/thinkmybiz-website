SARS notices

Received a letter from SARS? Here is what to do.

Verification requests, audit letters, assessments, penalties and final demands all have their own procedures and deadlines. Acting early keeps your options open.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

1. Send us the full notice straight away

Send the complete notice, the date you received it, the relevant tax type and period, and any existing case reference. Ask us for a secure route before sending sensitive supporting documents.

2. We identify what SARS is asking and the deadline

We review SARS notices, returns, accounts and supporting records, identify the disputed issues and the appropriate procedural route. Each case has its own filing and escalation deadlines.

3. We prepare the right response

Verification or audit letter
We prepare evidence packs, reconcile figures and respond to SARS information requests and proposed findings.
Assessment you disagree with
A Request for Correction where available, a request for reasons, or a Notice of Objection with grounds and evidence; an appeal or ADR where there is a basis.
Penalties and interest
Requests for remission where legislation permits.
Debt or final demand
We check the balance, then prepare a payment arrangement, a section 164 suspension request or a compromise application where the criteria may be met.
Delayed refund or unresolved query
Account queries, SARS complaints escalation and Tax Ombud submissions where the requirements are met.

Important

An objection or appeal does not by itself suspend payment; suspension must be considered separately. Payment terms, suspension, remission, compromise and VDP relief are subject to eligibility and SARS approval. We do not promise debt write-offs or a particular outcome.
