Services

Accounting and financial reporting

Reliable accounting gives business owners a usable view of performance and supports tax filings, finance applications and year-end reporting. We offer ongoing services and once-off catch-up work.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Bookkeeping and monthly accounts

  • Capture and classification of transactions from bank statements, invoices and supporting records.
  • Bank, cash and control-account reconciliations, with queries raised for missing or unclear transactions.
  • Debtor and creditor schedules, collection information and supplier reconciliations.
  • Fixed-asset registers, inventory-accounting support and payroll integration where applicable.
  • Monthly or periodic management accounts, with explanations of material movements.
  • Historic bookkeeping recovery and year-end adjustment schedules.

Annual financial statements

We compile annual financial statements and related working papers for companies, close corporations, sole proprietors, trusts and other entities within the agreed mandate. The reporting framework is determined by the entity and applicable requirements, including IFRS for SMEs where appropriate.

Services may include trial-balance review, reconciliations, disclosure schedules, tax computations and close corporation accounting officer work where the appointment and professional requirements are met. Statutory audit or independent review requirements are assessed separately and referred where necessary.

Management information

We explain profitability, cash requirements and outstanding balances so that owners can make informed decisions. Client books are kept on Taxnify, our accounting and compliance platform, with a full audit trail.

Resolving uncertain transactions

We maintain a clear list of missing evidence and transactions needing client explanation. Queries are resolved through supporting documents and agreed treatment before the affected reports or returns are finalised.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Bank statements for the period (PDF or CSV)
  • Sales and purchase invoices
  • Payroll reports, if you have staff
  • Previous financial statements and trial balance

Frequently asked questions

My books are a year or more behind. Can you catch up?

Yes. We offer once-off historic bookkeeping recovery from bank statements and supporting records, followed by year-end adjustments, financial statements and tax returns.

Do you prepare financial statements for close corporations?

Yes, including close corporation accounting officer work where the appointment and professional requirements are met.
