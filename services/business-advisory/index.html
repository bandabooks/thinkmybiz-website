Services

Business advisory and funding support

Advisory engagements connect recommendations to the owner's financial information and compliance obligations, so decisions are made on reliable numbers.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

What advisory work covers

  • Business structure and start-up registration needs: which entity, which registrations, and in what order.
  • Pricing and budgets that reflect your real costs, VAT position and tax.
  • Cash-flow planning and forecasts, including provision for SARS payments.
  • Internal recordkeeping that makes monthly accounting and tax filing straightforward.
  • Preparation for finance or tender opportunities.

Management information and funding support

We prepare budgets, cash-flow forecasts, business plans and financial information for funding applications. We explain profitability, cash requirements and outstanding balances so that owners can make informed decisions.

Advice tied to your numbers

Recommendations are based on your financial information and compliance obligations. Legal drafting and regulated investment advice require the appropriate professional mandate.

Frequently asked questions

I am starting a business. Should it be a company or a sole proprietorship?

We look at your plans, expected income, risks and tax position, and explain the registration and compliance obligations of each option before you decide.

Can you prepare financials for a funding application?

Yes. We prepare budgets, cash-flow forecasts, business plans and the financial information lenders and funders ask for.
