Services

CIPC, registrations, licences and tenders

We support the administrative requirements that sit alongside the accounts and tax returns, helping owners maintain consistent information across the relevant authorities.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

CIPC and company administration

  • Company registration and company-information maintenance.
  • Annual returns and the associated financial information or accountability submissions where required.
  • Beneficial ownership filings and supporting ownership information.
  • Director, registered-office and other company-detail changes.
  • Maintenance of agreed company records and assistance with deregistration or reinstatement processes where appropriate.

Registrations, licences and application support

We assist with Central Supplier Database registration and profile maintenance, tender compliance packs and financial documents, and relevant business applications. Support can include CIDB, PSIRA and licence-related documentation within an agreed administrative scope. Approvals and licensing decisions remain with the relevant authority.

B-BBEE affidavits

We assist with B-BBEE affidavit documentation where the entity qualifies. Where a verification certificate is required, the work is referred to an appropriately accredited verification provider.

A defined engagement

The quotation identifies deliverables, exclusions, responsibilities and fees; external approval and third-party charges are addressed separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can you register a new company for me?

Yes. We handle company registration with CIPC, and can then register the company for the taxes it needs and set up its books.

We want to tender for government work. Where do we start?

We assist with CSD registration and profile maintenance, tax compliance status, and the financial and compliance documents in a tender pack, including CIDB or PSIRA documentation where relevant.
