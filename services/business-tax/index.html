Services

Business and trust tax

We align taxable income with the underlying financial records and the requirements of the relevant taxpayer. Company, trust and owner obligations are considered separately.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Companies and close corporations

  • Corporate income tax registration and eFiling administration, including representative-taxpayer updates and tax-type transfers.
  • Preparation and submission of ITR14 returns and supporting tax computations.
  • Reconciliation of accounting profit to taxable income, with schedules for relevant deductions, allowances and assessed losses.
  • Review of small business corporation eligibility and turnover tax suitability where applicable.
  • Review of shareholder and director transactions, loan accounts, remuneration and distributions.
  • Dividends tax declarations, withholding administration and exemption documentation where applicable.
  • Tax Compliance Status (TCS) reviews and good-standing PIN requests, with assistance to address non-compliance.
  • Dormant-company filing reviews, historic-return catch-up and tax deregistration support when appropriate.

Trusts and beneficiaries

We assist with SARS trust registration, ITR12T returns, financial and tax schedules, beneficiary allocations and the supporting records for trustee decisions. Work can include IT3(t) reporting and reconciliation of distributions to the affected beneficiaries' tax information, where required.

Trust tax is considered alongside the trust deed, letters of authority and trustee resolutions. Legal establishment, deed amendments and complex trust structures require the appropriate legal input.

Nonprofit and exempt organisations

We provide tax-registration and compliance support for nonprofit organisations, and assistance with public benefit organisation and section 18A applications where the organisation qualifies. We distinguish CIPC or nonprofit registration from SARS tax-exempt approval and assess ongoing filing and record requirements.

A scope suited to the entity

We confirm the entity type, tax registrations, reporting period and applicable requirements before preparation. Exemptions, incentives and special tax regimes depend on statutory eligibility.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Company or trust registration documents
  • Latest financial statements or trial balance
  • Prior-year returns and SARS correspondence
  • Shareholder or beneficiary loan account details
  • Dividend declarations and trustee resolutions, where relevant

Frequently asked questions

My company has years of outstanding returns. Can you help?

Yes. We review the company's filing history, catch up historic returns and help address non-compliance so that a tax compliance status PIN can be obtained.

Do you handle trusts?

Yes: SARS trust registration, ITR12T returns, beneficiary allocations and IT3(t) reporting, considered alongside the trust deed and trustee resolutions.
