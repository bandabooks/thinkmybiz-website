Foreign income, expat and tax residency

South Africans who work or live abroad, and foreign nationals earning South African income, often face different tax rules. We assess your position and handle the SARS side.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

What we help with

  • Assessing whether you are a South African tax resident in a given year, based on your facts and the tests in the Income Tax Act.
  • Declaring foreign employment income on your ITR12, and claiming the foreign employment income exemption where the requirements are met.
  • Claiming credits for foreign tax paid, with the supporting certificates SARS requires.
  • Ceasing South African tax residency: assessing the date, the tax consequences, and the notification to SARS.
  • Non-residents with South African income, such as rental income, and their filing obligations.
  • Coordination with specialists in the other country, or for complex treaty questions.

Why early advice helps

Residency and foreign income rules turn on dates, days present and documents. Getting the position right before a return is filed, or before you leave, avoids estimated assessments, double taxation and penalties later.

Specialist input where needed

Foreign tax returns, treaty disputes and complex structures are coordinated with appropriately qualified specialists in the relevant country; responsibilities and any external fees are agreed first.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Your passport travel dates for the relevant tax years
  • Foreign employment contracts and payslips
  • Foreign tax returns or certificates of tax paid
  • Your SARS tax number and recent assessments

Frequently asked questions

I work overseas. Do I still need to file in South Africa?

It depends on whether you are still a South African tax resident and on your income. We assess your residency and filing obligations from your travel dates and employment details.

Can I stop being a South African tax resident?

Yes, ceasing residency is possible, but it has tax consequences on the date it happens. We assess the date and the consequences and handle the SARS notification.
