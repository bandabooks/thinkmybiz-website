Services

Individual and provisional tax

We assist employees, directors, independent contractors, sole proprietors, landlords and other individual taxpayers with their South African tax affairs, from registration through to assessment review.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Personal income tax

  • Income tax registration, registered-detail updates and authorised eFiling administration.
  • Preparation and submission of individual income tax returns (ITR12), including review of prepopulated information and auto-assessments.
  • Reconciliation of IRP5 and IT3 certificates, investment income, retirement contributions and medical tax information.
  • Rental income and expenses, sole-proprietor income, commission income and other taxable receipts.
  • Travel allowance and business-travel schedules, including conversion and review of electronic logbooks against SARS record requirements.
  • Assessment checks, refund follow-ups, supporting-document submissions and corrections where the appropriate process permits.

Provisional tax

We prepare provisional tax calculations and IRP6 submissions for qualifying individuals, companies and trusts. This includes first and second estimates, consideration of additional payments, reconciliation to annual tax and reminders of the relevant payment dates.

Sole proprietors and independent earners

We compile income and expense schedules from business records, separate private expenditure, review allowable deductions and connect the business results to the owner's personal return. We also help identify VAT and employer-registration obligations as the business grows.

What you receive

An agreed return or tax calculation, a supporting-document request, the amount and timing of any payment, and submission evidence. Final liability and refund processing remain subject to SARS assessment and review.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Your SARS tax number and ID (sent through a secure route)
  • IRP5/IT3(a) certificates from every employer
  • IT3(b)/IT3(c) investment certificates
  • Retirement annuity and medical aid tax certificates
  • Rental, freelance or business income and expense records
  • Travel logbook, if you receive a travel allowance

Frequently asked questions

Can you check an auto-assessment before I accept it?

Yes. We review the prepopulated information and auto-assessment against your IRP5, IT3 and other certificates, and add income or deductions that were left out where the process permits.

I earn rental or freelance income. Can you include it?

Yes. We prepare schedules for rental income and expenses, sole-proprietor, commission and other taxable receipts, and connect them to your personal return.

Do I need to come to the office?

No. Documents are sent electronically and we meet by phone or video call, wherever you are in South Africa.
