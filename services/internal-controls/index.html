Services

SME internal controls and financial health review

A practical, fixed-scope review of how money and compliance are controlled in your business, followed by a short report of what to fix first.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

What we review

Cash and banking
Who can pay, approve and change bank details; reconciliation of bank accounts; petty cash.
Sales and debtors
Invoicing, credit notes and follow-up of overdue accounts.
Purchases and creditors
Supplier set-up, approvals and duplicate-payment risk.
Payroll
Changes to employees and pay, approvals, and reconciliation to EMP201 and EMP501.
VAT and SARS
VAT201 workings, eFiling access, outstanding returns and SARS account status.
Records
Whether documents can be found quickly for a SARS verification or audit.

What you receive

A short written report with findings ranked by risk, practical fixes that suit the size of the business, and an offer to help implement them.

Not an audit

This review is an advisory engagement and does not result in an audit or review opinion. Statutory audits are performed by registered auditors and are referred where required.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Your bank statements and access list for banking and eFiling
  • Payroll reports for the last 2-3 months
  • Your latest VAT201 and EMP201 submissions
  • A short description of who does what in the finance function

Frequently asked questions

Is this the same as an audit?

No. It is a practical advisory review of controls; it does not give an audit opinion. Where a statutory audit is required, we refer you to a registered auditor.

How long does it take?

The scope and timing are agreed upfront in the quotation, based on the size of the business.
