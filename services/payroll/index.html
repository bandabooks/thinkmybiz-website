Services

Payroll and employer taxes

We help employers connect payroll calculations, employee certificates and SARS declarations, with reconciliation across the monthly and employer-reconciliation cycles.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Payroll processing

  • Payroll setup and employee-data maintenance, with agreed controls over changes.
  • Salary and wage calculations, payslips and payroll reporting.
  • Treatment of overtime, allowances, benefits, deductions and leave-related payments using employer-approved inputs.
  • Payroll journals and reconciliation of payroll balances to the accounting records.

Employer registrations and returns

  • PAYE, UIF and Skills Development Levy registration and administration where required.
  • EMP201 declarations and payment reconciliations.
  • Interim and annual EMP501 employer reconciliations.
  • Preparation, reconciliation and issue of IRP5 and IT3(a) employee certificates.
  • Employment Tax Incentive eligibility reviews, calculations and supporting records where applicable.
  • Tax-directive application support for qualifying payments, with specialist input for complex cases.

Employer compliance recovery

We investigate mismatches between payroll, submitted declarations, payments and employee certificates. We assist with outstanding reconciliations, correction of certificates and responses to SARS employer-tax queries, penalties and assessments.

Related labour administration

Related support includes UIF employer administration and declarations, Compensation Fund registration and returns of earnings, and assistance with letters of good standing. These processes are managed through the relevant authorities and are distinct from SARS tax filings.

Employer approval and records

The employer confirms employee details, remuneration and payroll changes. We agree cut-off dates, filing responsibilities and payment instructions in the engagement.

Frequently asked questions

Our EMP501 does not balance. Can you fix it?

Yes. We investigate mismatches between payroll, EMP201 declarations, payments and IRP5 certificates, and prepare the corrections and outstanding reconciliations.

Do you help with UIF and the Compensation Fund?

Yes: UIF employer administration and declarations, Compensation Fund registration and returns of earnings, and letters of good standing.
