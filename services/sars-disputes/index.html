SARS disputes and resolution

A disputed assessment requires the correct procedure, supporting evidence and careful deadline management. We review the facts and the available remedy before preparing a response.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

How we handle a dispute

Assessment and case review
Review SARS notices, returns, accounts and supporting records; identify the disputed issues and the appropriate procedural route.
Verification and audit responses
Prepare evidence packs, reconcile figures and respond to SARS information requests and proposed findings.
Corrections and reasons
Use a Request for Correction where available, or request reasons needed to understand and challenge an assessment or qualifying decision.
Objections
Prepare and lodge a Notice of Objection with grounds, relevant calculations and supporting evidence for an objectionable assessment or decision.
Appeals
Review an objection outcome and prepare a Notice of Appeal where there is a basis to pursue the matter.
Alternative dispute resolution
Assist with ADR preparation and participation where the dispute qualifies and SARS agrees to the process.
Late submissions
Assess whether an extension or condonation is legally available and prepare the motivation and evidence.
Outcome implementation
Review the decision, revised assessment, account adjustments and any remaining steps or payment obligations.

Penalties and interest

We prepare requests for remission of administrative or late-payment penalties and interest where legislation permits, and assess further remedies following the outcome. Understatement penalties are reviewed with the facts, disclosure history and applicable grounds. The available remedy differs by tax type and decision.

Act as soon as a notice arrives

Send the complete notice and the date you received it. Each case has its own filing and escalation deadlines. An objection or appeal does not by itself suspend payment; suspension must be considered separately. Court proceedings are coordinated with legal practitioners where required.

Frequently asked questions

SARS has issued an assessment I disagree with. What now?

Send us the full notice and the date you received it. We review the facts and choose the right route: a request for correction, a request for reasons, or a notice of objection, followed by an appeal or ADR where there is a basis.

Does lodging an objection stop SARS collecting the debt?

No. An objection or appeal does not by itself suspend payment. A separate suspension of payment request must be considered, and we can prepare it where it is available.

Can you help if I missed the deadline to object?

We assess whether an extension or condonation is legally available and, if so, prepare the motivation and evidence.
