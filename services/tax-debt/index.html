Tax debt and compliance recovery

We first establish whether the amount is correct, whether returns are outstanding and whether the taxpayer disputes the liability or needs assistance with payment. These questions determine the next step.

SARS account and debt reconciliation

We reconcile assessments, penalties, interest, payments and credits; investigate unallocated payments and incorrect balances; and prepare account queries with supporting proof. Outstanding returns and reconciliations are identified as part of the recovery plan.

Payment arrangements
Prepare instalment or deferral requests, cash-flow information and supporting motivations; review terms and assist with monitoring agreed payments.
Suspension of payment
Assess and prepare a section 164 application for disputed tax where available, with evidence supporting the request and follow-up on the outcome.
Debt compromise
Assess eligibility and prepare an application and financial disclosure for SARS consideration where the statutory criteria can be met.
Demand and collection notices
Review notices, reconcile the debt and assist with the appropriate administrative response. Urgent enforcement or court matters are referred for legal assistance.

Voluntary disclosure and historic defaults

We assess whether a historic default may qualify for the permanent Voluntary Disclosure Programme, help assemble a complete disclosure and coordinate specialist input where required. Routine corrections, VDP relief and debt compromise are separate procedures with different requirements.

Service complaints and the Tax Ombud

We assist with delayed refunds, unresolved account queries and administrative or procedural complaints. This can include SARS complaints escalation and a Tax Ombud submission where the mandate and access requirements are met. Complaints do not replace objections or appeals against the merits of an assessment.

Decisions rest with SARS

Payment terms, suspension, remission, compromise and VDP relief are subject to eligibility and SARS approval. Interest and recovery consequences must be considered while an application is pending. We do not promise debt write-offs or a particular outcome.

Frequently asked questions

I owe SARS money I cannot pay at once. Can you help?

Yes. We first check that the balance is correct, then prepare an instalment or deferral request with cash-flow information and supporting motivation for SARS to consider.

Can you get my tax debt written off?

We do not promise write-offs. Where the statutory criteria may be met, we assess eligibility for a compromise and prepare the application; the decision rests with SARS.

My refund has been delayed for months. What can be done?

We follow up the account query and, where the requirements are met, escalate through SARS complaints and the Tax Ombud.
