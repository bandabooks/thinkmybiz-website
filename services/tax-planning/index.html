Services

Tax planning and specialist support

We help clients understand the tax consequences of business and personal decisions before transactions are concluded. Advice is based on the facts and the applicable law, with specialist coordination where the matter requires it.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Areas we advise on

Capital gains tax
Calculate and report relevant gains and losses on disposals, including property, business assets and investments, with applicable exclusions and relief considered.
Property and rental tax
Review rental schedules, property disposals and the interaction of VAT, income tax and capital gains tax; coordinate transfer-duty matters with the conveyancer.
Dividends and withholding taxes
Review distributions and relevant withholding obligations, exemption declarations and treaty documentation where applicable.
Donations and estate matters
Assist with donations tax calculations and reporting, and deceased-estate tax compliance and estate-duty support in coordination with the executor and legal advisers.
Cross-border tax
Initial assessment of foreign income, residency changes, foreign tax credits, non-resident obligations and international-transfer tax documentation, with specialist support as needed.
Transaction planning
Assess owner remuneration, asset acquisitions, business structures, disposals and succession proposals before implementation.
Incentives and exemptions
Review qualifying allowances, small-business regimes and available exemptions with the required eligibility evidence.

Additional specialist tax coordination

For customs and excise, transfer pricing, securities transfer tax, mining royalties, carbon tax, global minimum tax and other sector-specific taxes or levies, we assess the requirement and coordinate appropriately qualified specialists. Acceptance of a specialist mandate depends on the expertise, authorisation and scope required.

Advice before implementation

Legal drafting, conveyancing, regulated investment advice and court representation require the appropriate professional mandate. We agree responsibilities and any external specialist fees before proceeding.

What to send us

To get started, these are usually enough. We will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • A short description of the planned transaction or decision
  • Agreements, valuations or offers, if available
  • Recent financial statements and tax returns

Frequently asked questions

I am selling a property. Should I speak to you first?

Yes. We review the disposal and the interaction of income tax, capital gains tax and VAT before the sale, and coordinate transfer-duty matters with the conveyancer.

How should I pay myself from my company?

We assess owner remuneration, dividends and loan accounts against the company's position and your personal tax before you implement a structure.
