Services

VAT services

We support VAT vendors through registration, periodic returns, reconciliations, SARS queries and changes to the business. Good source records are central to a defensible VAT position.

Book a free consultation Call 066 210 5631

Registration and administration

  • Compulsory and voluntary VAT registration assessments and applications.
  • Supporting-document packs, SARS registration queries and follow-up.
  • Registered-detail and banking-detail updates through the applicable verification process.
  • Tax-period reviews and VAT deregistration applications, including consideration of closing adjustments.

Returns and VAT reconciliations

  • Preparation and submission of VAT201 returns from accounting records.
  • Reconciliation of output tax to sales, and input tax to qualifying purchases and supporting tax invoices.
  • Review of standard-rated, zero-rated, exempt and out-of-scope transactions, as applicable.
  • Consideration of credit notes, debit notes, bad-debt adjustments, capital assets, mixed-use expenditure and apportionment.
  • Reconciliation of VAT control accounts and SARS statements, including payment-allocation and refund queries.

Verification and dispute support

We assemble transaction schedules and invoices, respond to VAT verification and audit requests, review findings and assist with the applicable correction, objection or appeal process. Where SARS has issued an estimated assessment, we assess the prescribed route for supplying the missing information and seeking a revised assessment.

Practical VAT advice

We explain how VAT affects quotations, invoicing, cash flow and recordkeeping. VAT charged to customers must be accounted for correctly, and input tax claims require the relevant legal and documentary support.

Frequently asked questions

SARS is verifying my VAT return. What should I do?

Send us the SARS letter as soon as you receive it. We assemble the transaction schedules and invoices SARS asks for and respond within the stated deadline.

Should my business register for VAT?

We assess whether registration is compulsory or whether voluntary registration makes sense for your business, and prepare the application and supporting documents.
